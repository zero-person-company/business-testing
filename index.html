Scope, limitations & important context
  • This is a founder-informed, unvalidated prototype. There is no independent expert validation, demonstrated demand, revenue or demonstrated savings.
  • It does not replace subscriptions, combine customer data, connect to services, cancel anything, purchase anything or take action automatically. A person must review candidates.
  • Unused cost is an estimate of current monthly-equivalent spend, not actual or recoverable savings. Multiple tools in a category are a prompt to review, not proof of redundancy.
  • No integrations, cancellations, CRM, lead generation, customer records, payments, sales promises or validated pricing. The founder's $500/month estimate is opinion, not a price.
  • Only subscription metadata belongs here. Data is held in current-tab memory, is not saved or sent anywhere, and is lost when the tab is closed or refreshed. No external requests are made.
  • This narrow audit does not test an all-in-one service or a half-price hypothesis. No explicit privacy constraints or disconfirming evidence were supplied.
