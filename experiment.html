Vehicle Service Visit Prep Card

Collect your own notes before contacting a technician or attending a service appointment. This worksheet organizes observations and questions; it does not interpret them.

Practical limitsThis tool does not diagnose vehicle problems, recommend repairs, or replace a technician's assessment. No service, follow-up, or individualized advice is included. If you think the vehicle is unsafe to operate, do not rely on this worksheet to decide what to do.

Privacy: avoid entering sensitive information such as your full address, account details, or personal identifiers. Your entries stay in this tab and are not saved by this page. Closing or reloading the tab discards them.
